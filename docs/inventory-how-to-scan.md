# Inventory: how to scan

Open **app.bates-electric.com/inventory**, or tap the menu (top left), then **Inventory**. Techs also have an **Inventory** card on their home screen. The first time you scan, your phone asks to use the camera. Tap **Allow**.

## Scanning something

1. Tap **Scan** on the inventory screen. The camera opens with an aiming box. It doesn't read anything yet.
2. Put the box over **one** barcode, about 6 inches away.
3. Tap the big yellow **Scan** button at the bottom. The phone beeps, shows the code and opens the item.
4. Two barcodes in the box? It asks which one. Tap the right one.
5. "No barcode found"? Move closer, or tap the **flashlight** (Android only; iPhones don't allow it in the browser), then tap **Scan** again.
6. Barcode torn or missing? Type the number or part # in the box at the bottom and tap **Go**.
7. Have a USB or Bluetooth scanner? Just scan with it on any inventory screen. No need to tap anything first.

## Taking things out (warehouse + techs)

1. Scan the item. Its page opens.
2. Set how many with **−** / **+**, then tap **− Out**.
3. "For what?" is optional: type the job, or search a Generator Care customer. Tap **Scan out**.
4. Next: tap **Scan next** to go straight to the camera, or **Back to inventory**.
5. Serial-numbered gear (ATSs): tap **− Scan out unit**, then pick the serial number or scan its label.

## Tapped the wrong thing? Undo

After **+ In**, **− Out**, **Count** or a scan-out, an **Undo** bar shows at the bottom for 10 seconds. Tap it.

Missed it? Your own latest change on an item has **Undo this** in its History for 15 minutes. Both lines stay in the history so everyone can see what happened.

## Receiving and fixing counts (warehouse)

- **+ In**: stock came in. Set how many, tap **+ In**.
- **Count**: the number on screen is wrong. Type what's really on the shelf.
- **Receive unit** (serial-numbered gear): scan or type the serial number.
- **New item**: scan a barcode we don't have and a short form opens (no barcode at all? **More**, then **New item**). Only a **description** and **quantity** are required. Tap **Take photo**, then **Save item**.

## The first big count: Bulk count (warehouse)

1. Home screen, then **Bulk count** ("Scan many items, then save all at once"), then **Start bulk count**.
2. Pick how the camera reads. The phone remembers your choice.
   - **Tap to scan** (normal): aim at one barcode, tap **Scan**, and repeat for each one.
   - **Continuous**: fast counting of one item at a time. Every barcode you hold in the box adds 1. Move off it and back to count the next box.
3. Tap **Scan** and go shelf by shelf.
4. Something with no barcode? Use **Search to add +1**.
5. A barcode we don't know opens the new-item form, then you're back to scanning.
6. Serial-numbered gear: scan the product barcode, then the serial number label.
7. If an item was counted in an earlier session, the phone asks: **Add to it** (more of the same somewhere else) or **Replace it** (this count is the real number).
8. Fix any number with **−** / **+** in the list. When done, tap **Save all**.

## Bad signal? Keep going

Everything saves on the phone first. While anything is waiting, the top of the screen says so (for example **"Offline · 12 waiting to sync"**); tap it to see what's waiting, the last error, and **Retry now**. It sends by itself when you're back on Wi-Fi or cell, and it never counts anything twice. Don't clear the browser while scans are waiting.

If you see a red **"not saved"** button, tap it. It lists what was refused or kept failing, and why (for example "only 2 on hand"). Nothing is thrown away: **Retry** sends it again, **Dismiss** removes it.

## Surplus (warehouse)

On an item, set **Surplus qty** (how many we're selling; the rest is "keep") and the **asking price**, then tap **Save surplus**. Sold or scrapped some? Tap **Sell / dispose**. **More**, then **Surplus list**, has the list with **CSV** and **PDF with photos** downloads for dealers.
