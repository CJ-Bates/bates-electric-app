# Inventory: how to scan

Open **app.bates-electric.com/inventory**, or tap the menu (top left), then **Inventory**. Techs also have an **Inventory** card on their home screen. The first time you scan, your phone asks to use the camera. Tap **Allow**.

## Scanning something

1. Tap the big **Scan** button.
2. Hold the barcode inside the white box, about 6 inches away. The phone beeps and the box flashes green.
3. If it's dark, tap the **flashlight** button (Android only; iPhones don't allow it in the browser).
4. Barcode torn or missing? Type the number or part # in the box at the bottom and tap **Go**.
5. Have a USB or Bluetooth scanner? Just scan with it on any inventory screen. No need to tap anything first.

## Taking things out (warehouse + techs)

1. Scan the item. Its page opens.
2. Set how many with **−** / **+**, then tap **− Out**.
3. "For what?" is optional: type the job, or search a Generator Care customer. Tap **Scan out**.
4. Serial-numbered gear (ATSs): tap **− Scan out unit**, then pick the serial number or scan its label.

## Receiving and fixing counts (warehouse)

- **+ In**: stock came in. Set how many, tap **+ In**.
- **Count**: the number on screen is wrong. Type what's really on the shelf.
- **Receive unit** (serial-numbered gear): scan or type the serial number.
- **New item**: scan a barcode we don't have and a short form opens. Only a **description** and **quantity** are required. Tap **Take photo**, then **Save item**.

## The first big count (warehouse)

1. Home screen, then **Count session**, then **Start count session**.
2. Tap **Scan** and go shelf by shelf. Each scan adds 1. To count the same box again, move off the barcode and back.
3. Something with no barcode? Use **Search to add +1**.
4. A barcode we don't know opens the new-item form, then you're back to scanning.
5. Serial-numbered gear: scan the product barcode, then the serial number label.
6. If an item was counted in an earlier session, the phone asks: **Add to it** (more of the same somewhere else) or **Replace it** (this count is the real number).
7. Fix any number with **−** / **+** in the list. When done, tap **Finish & post**.

## Bad signal? Keep going

Everything saves on the phone first. The top of the screen says **"Offline · 12 waiting to sync"**. It sends by itself when you're back on Wi-Fi or cell, and it never counts anything twice. Don't clear the browser while scans are waiting.

If you see a red **"not saved"** button, tap it. It lists the change the server refused and why, for example "only 2 on hand." Redo it if it's still needed.

## Surplus (warehouse)

On an item, set **Surplus qty** (how many we're selling; the rest is "keep") and the **asking price**, then tap **Save surplus**. Sold or scrapped some? Tap **Sell / dispose**. The **Surplus** page has the list with **CSV** and **PDF with photos** downloads for dealers.
